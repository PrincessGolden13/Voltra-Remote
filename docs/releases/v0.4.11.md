# VOLTRA Remote v0.4.11 — Connection handoff and Retry

Remove the misleading “Bluetooth may be in use” explanation. Connection failures
now report the stage and available stack code, with a direct Retry button.
The failure does not prove a phone is connected.

- Recognize the newly observed follower status for read-only master discovery,
  avoiding its unnecessary six-second wait before checking the other VOLTRA.
- Remember which member to check first from fresh role evidence. Every connection
  revalidates its role; device names, colors and scan order never choose the master.
- Wait for the previous Bluetooth attempt's terminal callback and cleared handle
  before reusing the client. A late callback cannot cancel the newly queued member.
- Open the freshly identified master's read-only weight screen. Preserve the
  paired names and overlapping circles without redundant status captions.
- When checking for updates offline, retry the saved Wi-Fi network before
  checking GitHub. A disconnected network does not mean its details were erased.
  This does not reset or replace saved credentials.

**Twin weight adjustment, load, unload and Auto Load remain unavailable on the
remote. Use the VOLTRAs' own controls.** The new role profile supports discovery
and read-only presentation; it does not establish Twin resistance-command support.
Physical Bluetooth handoff behavior still needs verification on the remote.

Unload both VOLTRAs and leave Bluetooth, then Settings -> Updates -> CHECK FOR
UPDATES -> INSTALL & RESTART. Keep power connected. If connection fails after
installing, Retry is available; Send debug log records the stage/code and timing.
