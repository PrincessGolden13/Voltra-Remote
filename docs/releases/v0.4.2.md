Rest completion alerts for the Guition JC3636K518 remote.

- When the configured rest time is reached after a set, vibrate for up to 20
  seconds and show a Rest over button. Tap it to stop early and dismiss.
- After vibration stops automatically, keep Rest over visible and the screen
  awake until acknowledged. The button persists across screen navigation.
- Detect and drive the optional DRV2605/2605L haptic chip on the existing I2C
  bus. No vibration at boot; a missing chip leaves the visual notification.
- Rest alert defaults to Off on first use and factory reset. Saved choices remain.

The new driver compiles for both public firmware targets; its physical vibration
strength, timing and touch behavior need confirmation on the owner's remote.
The owner's factory timer has been confirmed capable of vibrating this unit.
See docs/REST_ALERT_2026_11_09.md for evidence and pending checks.

Development preview: fresh-owner BLE pairing remains unresolved. Existing local
profiles remain in remote NVS; public builds contain no private profile. Ordinary
updates require the A/B layout already installed. Leave training before updating.
