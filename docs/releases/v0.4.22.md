# VOLTRA Remote v0.4.22

- The connection screen shows the VOLTRA names, including both members of a
  known Twin while the remote finds its controller.
- Twin recovery now scans for the reported controller's exact Bluetooth address
  after the follower connection closes and during existing retry gaps. A matching
  advertisement can refresh the radio route if the controller starts advertising
  during the handoff.
- Live always labels the set and rep counts. It accepts the controller's extended
  Twin counter stream as well as the shorter Twin runtime stream, including
  after Auto Load calibration. Counts appear as `--` until reported.

The hidden-controller recovery and Live counters still need a physical check.
Twin live target resistance remains unavailable pending a verified device report.

Unload the VOLTRAs and leave the connection. In **Settings → Updates**,
choose **Check for updates**, then **Install & Restart**. Keep power connected.
